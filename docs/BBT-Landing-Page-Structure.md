# BBT Thailand Bookstore — Landing Page Structure

Status: Agreed direction  
Primary goal: Help visitors browse Thai books  
Primary audience: Existing Thai devotees  
Secondary audience: International buyers

## 1. Landing Page Direction

The landing page should present BBT Thailand Bookstore as a modern independent
bookstore combined with a peaceful spiritual introduction and guided shopping
journey.

Visitors should immediately understand that:

- This is a bookstore specializing in BBT books.
- Books are available in Thai.
- English, Nepali, Hindi, and Russian editions are also available.
- The books are authentic BBT editions.
- Orders can be delivered worldwide.

The experience should feel:

- Trustworthy
- Peaceful
- Bright and energetic
- Modern and editorial
- Easy to browse

The website should avoid appearing:

- Old-fashioned
- Overly commercialized
- Overly religious
- Luxurious or exclusive

## 2. Visual Direction

Use a bright editorial bookstore style inspired by the supplied visual
references.

The design should include:

- Expressive editorial headline typography
- Clear and readable body typography
- Warm ivory backgrounds
- Bright, intentional color blocks
- The approved stepped plum-to-yellow color-band motif
- Large book covers as the primary imagery
- Slightly rotated and layered book compositions
- Generous whitespace
- Strong asymmetrical layouts balanced by a clean grid
- Simple black interface elements and fine divider lines

Use the supplied BBT logo in its original colors. Avoid decorative religious
clichés. The spiritual character should come primarily from the books, language,
editorial composition, and carefully selected copy.

Use simplified English spellings such as "Krishna" in customer-facing interface
copy unless an official book title uses diacritics.

## 3. Landing Page Sequence

1. Announcement bar
2. Header
3. Editorial hero
4. Trust strip
5. Featured Thai Books
6. New to Bhakti
7. About BBT
8. Find Your Way In (Languages + Topics)
9. Worldwide Delivery and Help
10. Final call to action
11. Footer

## 4. Announcement Bar

Recommended copy:

> Authentic BBT Editions · Based in Thailand · Worldwide Delivery

The announcement bar should establish trust. It should not look like a discount
or limited-time promotional banner.

## 5. Header

### Desktop navigation

- BBT logo
- Shop
- Thai Books
- Languages
- About BBT
- Search icon
- Cart icon with item count

"Thai Books" should have slightly stronger visual emphasis than the other
navigation links.

The header should remain slim and become sticky when visitors scroll.

### Mobile navigation

- Menu
- BBT logo
- Search icon
- Cart icon

The mobile menu should contain a prominent "Shop Thai Books" action.

## 6. Editorial Hero

### Headline

> Discover BBT Books in Thai

### Supporting copy

> Explore authentic Bhaktivedanta Book Trust editions in Thai, English, Nepali,
> Hindi, and Russian—delivered from Thailand to readers worldwide.

### Primary action

> Shop Thai Books →

The hero should have one dominant call to action. A secondary button is not
required.

### Hero imagery

- Bhagavad-gita should be the dominant featured book.
- Krishna Book should be the accompanying major work.
- Display the covers at contrasting angles.
- Place a small circular promotional or trust badge between the books.
- Use "Thai Editions" and "Worldwide Delivery" as badge messages.
- Include a small "5 Languages" credibility detail.
- Anchor the bottom of the hero with the stepped plum-to-yellow color bands.

The available English book covers may be used as temporary design assets. They
must not be labeled as Thai editions until the actual Thai cover images are
available.

Do not display prices or availability in the hero. The hero should remain
editorial and direct visitors to the catalog.

### Bhagavad-gita verse

Use this restrained excerpt from Bhagavad-gita 12.12:

> "By such renunciation one can attain peace of mind."

Source:
https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/12/12/

The quotation should be a small supporting detail rather than the hero's main
message.

## 7. Trust Strip

Display four concise trust points. These must NOT repeat the announcement bar
(§4). The bar states the promise; the trust strip gives the proof, using new
facts:

- Published by Bhaktivedanta Book Trust
- Ships from Bangkok
- Encrypted checkout
- Available in 5 languages

"Encrypted checkout" is a real claim. Use it only after the secure-payment
provider is confirmed (see §18). Until then, use "Tracked orders" instead.

Use simple typography or restrained icons. The section should appear factual
and reassuring rather than promotional.

## 8. Featured Thai Books

### Heading

> Featured Books in Thai

### Supporting copy

> Essential works of bhakti, self-knowledge, and spiritual wisdom in Thai.

Display exactly eight available Thai books.

### Product card content

Each product card should contain only:

- Book cover
- Title
- Price in Thai baht
- Availability status

The entire card should open the product page. A quick-add button and product
description are not needed.

Pre-order products may appear alongside available books but must have a clearly
visible, distinct "Pre-order" label.

### Grid

- Desktop: Four columns
- Tablet: Two or three columns, depending on available width
- Mobile: Two columns

### Section action

> View All Thai Books →

## 9. New to Bhakti

### Heading

> New to Bhakti? Begin Here.

This section should recommend one definitive starting book instead of presenting
a decision tree.

The recommended starting book is the Pocket Bhagavad-gita because it is
approachable, portable, and less expensive than the principal edition.

Include:

- Large book cover
- Short newcomer-friendly introduction
- Price
- A brief "Why begin here?" explanation
- "Discover This Book" button

The section should feel like personal guidance from a thoughtful bookseller,
not another product grid.

## 10. About BBT

### Heading

> Timeless Knowledge, Published for the World

Include one concise editorial paragraph explaining BBT's international
publishing work and the authenticity of its editions.

### Section action

> Learn About BBT →

The homepage section should establish authority and trust. Detailed history
belongs on the dedicated About BBT page.

This section is placed between the browsing blocks on purpose. It gives the eye
a rest and a reason to trust before the visitor is asked to browse again.

## 11. Find Your Way In

Merge languages and topics into ONE section with a single heading, shown as two
rows: languages on top, topics below. This turns two full-height sections into
one and shortens the scroll.

### Heading

> Find Your Way In

### Row 1 — Explore by Language

Use simple typographic cards rather than country flags.

Languages:

- Thai
- English
- Nepali
- Hindi
- Russian

Thai should be the largest and most prominent card. Each card may eventually
show the number of available books, but only after the catalog counts are
confirmed.

Each card should include:

- Language name
- Optional confirmed product count
- Directional arrow

### Row 2 — Browse by Topic

Use clear, newcomer-friendly English category names:

- Self & Consciousness
- Yoga & Meditation
- Karma & Reincarnation
- Sacred Stories
- Spiritual Wisdom
- Life's Big Questions

Use typography, color, and selected book-cover details instead of generic
spiritual icons.

## 12. Worldwide Delivery and Help

### Heading

> Books from Thailand, Delivered Worldwide

Include:

- A short explanation of worldwide shipping
- A clear link to shipping information
- An invitation to ask for help choosing a book

Suggested help copy:

> Not sure which book to begin with? We'll help you choose.

Specific carrier, cost, delivery-time, and destination promises must not be
published until the shipping policy is confirmed.

## 13. Final Call to Action

Reuse the stepped color-band motif more subtly than in the hero.

### Heading

> Find Your Next Book

### Action

> Explore Thai Books →

This section should return visitors to the landing page's primary goal.

## 14. Footer

Include:

- Shop links
- Language links
- About BBT
- About the Store
- Contact and help
- Shipping information
- Returns policy
- Privacy policy
- Terms and conditions
- Social channels when available
- Copyright and trademark wording
- Independent-reseller disclosure

The reseller disclosure does not need to appear beneath the logo or in the
announcement bar. It should still appear clearly in the footer and on the About
the Store page so the bookstore is not mistaken for the official BBT Thailand
organization.

## 15. Responsive Priorities

On smaller screens:

- Keep the headline readable without excessive wrapping.
- Stack the book-cover composition beneath or behind the hero copy.
- Preserve the stepped color-band motif without making it too tall.
- Keep "Shop Thai Books" prominent.
- Use two-column product grids where book titles remain readable.
- Make all product cards, navigation controls, and calls to action comfortable
  to use by touch.
- Reduce decorative elements before reducing essential product information.

## 16. Customer Journey

The intended landing-page journey is:

> Understand the store → Trust it → Browse Thai books → Receive beginner
> guidance → Explore other languages and topics → Learn about BBT → Shop
