# BBT Thailand Online Bookstore — Website Idea Document

Status: Discovery draft  
Prepared: 24 July 2026  
Primary market: Thailand

## 1. Project Summary

Create a trustworthy independent e-commerce bookstore specializing in Bhaktivedanta Book Trust titles for customers in Thailand and worldwide. The store will make BBT titles easy to discover and purchase, with Thai-language books given the strongest homepage visibility while also offering English, Nepali, Hindi, and Russian editions.

The experience should support both newcomers looking for an accessible introduction and existing readers searching for specific scriptures, volumes, languages, and editions.

The business is an independent reseller, so the website must describe that relationship clearly and must not imply that it is the official BBT Thailand organization unless written authorization permits that representation.

## 2. Confirmed Context

- BBT is an international publisher.
- BBT Thailand is the local focus of this project.
- Thai is the primary language and commercial focus.
- The initial storefront interface will be in English.
- Books in English, Nepali, Hindi, and Russian will also be available.
- The supplied VedaBase library contains 27 reference titles.
- Some works contain multiple volumes or parts, including Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam and Śrīla Prabhupāda-līlāmṛta.
- The international BBT publishes traditional scriptures and books about bhakti-yoga in many languages and formats.
- Currency for the Thailand store will be Thai baht (THB / ฿).
- Customers will be a mixture of Thai newcomers, existing devotees, temples or community members, and international/expatriate readers; the priority order is not yet confirmed.
- Version one will serve individual retail customers rather than wholesale customers.
- Worldwide delivery will be offered.
- The reseller has permission to use the BBT name and supplied logo.
- The final public store name has not yet been selected.
- The launch catalog will include every physical book currently available across Thai, English, Nepali, Hindi, and Russian.
- Each language edition will be a separate product.
- Multi-volume works may be purchased as individual volumes and as complete sets.
- The store will sell physical books only.
- Coming-soon products will support pre-order purchasing.

## 5. Proposed Homepage Direction

1. An editorial hero featuring the Thai Bhagavad-gītā and one additional book.
2. A primary call to action to shop Thai books.
3. Featured Thai titles.
4. An “Explore by language” section.
5. A beginner-friendly “New to Bhakti? Start Here” section.
6. Featured scriptures, introductory books, and multi-volume collections.
7. A short section about BBT and its international publishing history.
8. Trust information: authentic editions, Thailand-based service, payment, and delivery.
9. Contact information, with the channels still to be selected.

### Hero composition

The hero will closely follow the supplied structural reference:

- Minimal header across the top
- Large, expressive editorial headline on the left
- Short supporting copy below the headline
- Bordered primary call-to-action button with a dark arrow block
- Thai Bhagavad-gītā cover displayed at a slight angle on the right
- One additional featured book displayed at a contrasting angle
- Small circular promotional or trust badge between the books
- A small credibility detail beneath the imagery, adapted to meaningful store content
- Stepped horizontal color bands anchoring the bottom of the hero
- Spacious off-white background and asymmetrical composition

The composition will be rebuilt with original store branding, copy, typography, colors, approved book covers, and interface details. It will not reproduce the reference site’s logo, wording, community avatars, or exact brand assets.

## 11. Visual Direction

### Overall direction

A modern editorial bookstore aesthetic: cultured, spacious, confident, and contemporary. It should feel like a carefully designed independent bookshop while remaining respectful of the spiritual subject matter.

### Qualities drawn from the supplied reference

- Generous white space and strong editorial composition
- Expressive display typography paired with a highly readable body typeface
- Large book covers used as the main visual content
- Slightly rotated or layered product imagery for energy
- Simple black interface controls and fine divider lines
- Bright color used in deliberate blocks, bands, labels, and calls to action
- Asymmetrical layouts balanced by a clean grid
- Magazine-like collection and category sections

### Original BBT interpretation

- Prioritize Thai-translated books in the hero and first product collection.
- Use a warm, literary palette that complements the supplied red logo without requiring red as the dominant website color.
- Consider warm ivory, ink black, muted botanical green, saffron or marigold, and one restrained devotional accent.
- Use the supplied stepped multicolor band as a recognizable graphic motif.
- Avoid making the site look like a generic corporate publisher, a crowded marketplace, or an imitation of the reference.
- Use authentic book covers, approved archival artwork, and restrained spiritual motifs rather than decorative clichés.
- Ensure Thai and Latin typography work harmoniously even though the initial interface is English.

### Stepped color-band motif

The supplied band design is an approved visual element for the website.

- Six horizontal steps rise from left to right.
- The color progression moves through deep plum, magenta, coral-red, orange, and golden yellow.
- A soft warm-ivory glow sits behind the upper steps.
- The motif must anchor the bottom of the homepage hero.
- It may also be reused selectively for major section transitions, promotional banners, or the footer.
- Secondary uses should be restrained so the motif retains impact.
- The design must remain crisp and geometric on desktop and adapt cleanly on mobile without becoming too tall.
