## Scope and mode

- Surface: post-checkout preview confirmation.
- Mode: Operate.
- Primary target: `thank-you.html`.
- Related targets: `css/thank-you.css`, `js/thank-you.js`, `js/checkout.js`.

## Audience and job

Retail book buyers need to know that the checkout preview finished, verify the captured books and destination, and choose what to do next.

## Truth and constraints

- Render locally captured cart items, quantities, subtotal, and city/country.
- Do not claim payment, stock reservation, server submission, carrier, rate, or delivery timing.
- State prominently that this is a design preview and no order or payment occurred.
- Preserve the Bright Reading Room world and real BBT assets.

## Direction

“Completed Spine”: the checkout’s five ruled steps resolve into an oversized editorial confirmation. The memorable moment is the red-underlined italic completion word meeting the active fifth marker and cropped numeral.

## Primary action

Continue browsing books. Reviewing checkout details is secondary.

## Responsive behavior

The vertical spine becomes a horizontal numbered index below 1120px. Labels leave before the numbered progress at mobile size; order facts stack without hiding product content.

## Unresolved

Replace preview language and local storage with real order, payment, notification, and delivery data only after those systems are confirmed.
